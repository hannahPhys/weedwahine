Cannabis and the body

Your skin is the largest organ of your body, but did you realise it’s also your body’s first line of defence against invasive toxins. The skin acts as a protective layer with the body’s highest density of cannabinoid receptors. Cannabinoid receptors sit on the cell surfaces, waiting for specific neurotransmitters (those found in cannabis) to bind them. Different types of receptors are found in other parts of the body, as well, in what is known as your endocannabinoid system (ECS).

Cannabinoids are able to interact with these receptors triggering natural body processes that can reduce inflammation, calm anxiety, and soothe skin. Your body is always naturally producing cannabinoids that react with these receptors but they could always use a little boost from the outside.

Cannabis holds the perfect ratio between omega 6 & omega 3 in hemp seed, a 3:1 balance which has a hydrating and plumping effect on the skin. Hemp oil also contains magnesium-rich chlorophyll. Chlorophyll is just what your skin needs during the winter thanks to its hydrating effects and ability to soothe itchy, red skin. Even though the hemp plant cannot have more than 0.3% THC to be legal there are still plenty of other cannabinoids in the plant that interact with your ECS system to support your wellbeing. Hemp seeds and oils can be consumed to improve your health and skincare from the inside out by providing the nutrients you ECS system needs to regulate your bodys balance. Also the more you keep your ECS system healthy the higher you can get off consuming THC.

While much more research needs to be done, the existing anecdotal evidence and limited studies point to CBD’s positive healing potential. In a study published in the Journal of Clinical Investigation, researchers found that CBD can help reduce acne by curbing the production of sebum that leads to unwanted breakouts. Another promising cannabinoid study published in the Journal of Experimental Medicine found that CBD substantially reduced chronic inflammation.
